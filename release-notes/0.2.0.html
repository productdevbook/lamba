Lamba 0.2.0

The Dock gets a toolbox. After updating, Lamba asks for your password once to update its helper (for fan control).

  • Convert: drop images, videos or a folder on the right island to shrink or convert them: JPEG, PNG, HEIC, WebP, AVIF; HEVC, MP4, and AV1 or WebM with Homebrew's ffmpeg. Results go to Downloads.
  • System status: CPU and memory gauges, chip, battery and SSD temperatures, fan speeds with Auto / Max / Custom control, and network use over the last hour with the apps using it most.
  • Clipboard history: the last 20 copies, kept in memory only, passwords skipped, one click to clear.
  • Recent downloads as a stack in the Dock.
  • Keyboard light: turns off after a notification, flashes at the lowest brightness at night (your hours), and its brightness is adjustable.
  • Fixed: Apps (Launchpad) no longer pulses forever after a click.
